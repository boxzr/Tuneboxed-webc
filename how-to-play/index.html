How to play

Learn it in 30 seconds

  1. Start a room

    Hit Host a battle and read the code out loud, or drop the link in chat.

  2. Pick your song

    Everyone picks a track that fits the vibe and dresses their boxer.

  3. Walkouts

    Each song plays its entrance. The bell rings once both boxers are in the ring.

  4. Votes are punches

    Type 1 or 2 in chat, or tap in the room. Every vote lands a punch, and three in a row is a combo.

  5. Knockout or decision

    Drain a song’s health and it hits the canvas. A close fight goes to decision.

  6. Your turn

    Give one song the first five votes and it is a knockout. Otherwise the fight goes to a decision after nine. Tap 1 or 2, or press the keys. Land three in a row for a combo.
